Derive the domain model from evidence and stakeholder language from DDD workshop and artifacts in Docs\DDD-Lab\ . The outputs are already provided in each phase based on the prompts and input from previous stages

Produce a domain landscape, ubiquitous language, candidate aggregates, invariants, domain events, bounded contexts and context map.

Challenge each boundary by testing:

- business ownership;
- decision authority;
- terminology;
- transactional consistency;
- change rate;
- data sensitivity;
- failure impact.

Do not create contexts that merely mirror CSV files or technical layers.
