# Prompt 07 — Evaluation and Challenge

Audit the application and proposal as a skeptical independent reviewer.

Test:

- repeatability;
- evidence traceability;
- handling of stale, missing, duplicate, aliased and conflicting data;
- safety and authority boundaries;
- false confidence and automation bias;
- performance and degraded mode;
- architecture-to-code consistency;
- residual risks and unresolved domain assumptions.

Fix engineering defects, but do not invent operational rules.
